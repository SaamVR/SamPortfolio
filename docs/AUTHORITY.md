# THE OPENING
## Cinematic portfolio: production council and master plan

**8 October 2026 · Creative direction and technical planning · Working practice name: FORM & FEEL**

The missing piece was a cinematic idea that becomes useful through interaction. A striking abstract object alone would not demonstrate your ability to design and build a complete product. The agreed direction makes the same visual language connect first impression, authentic work, creative choices, an editable brief and a truthful enquiry receipt.

**Recommendation: THE OPENING.** Three unequal, rigid charcoal leaves articulate around an already-visible project preview. Their open architectural corner, clipped diagonal and printed coral seam form the identity. Selecting Precise, Playful or Cinematic changes their stance and cadence, and saves a real direction preference in the brief. The story continues through a project-to-case-study image match and resolves in a usable document. The visual grammar is **Cut / Fold / Reveal**.

This is an original proposal informed by the ambition of Lusion's multidisciplinary presentation. It does not reproduce Lusion's compositions, assets or choreography. The earlier ribbon concept is deliberately replaced; its satin charcoal/coral material connection is retained. Earlier static mockup project examples are not publishable portfolio evidence.

## The experience: a visitor-directed screenplay

The visitor is the editor of the experience. There is no mandatory six-chapter tour. The following beats describe the compositional arc available during ordinary navigation.

FORM & FEEL remains a working name.

### Three signature sequences

**A — Spatial reframing.** Choose a feel; semantic selection and draft update immediately. In the shared 700ms animatic, the lead starts at 60ms, secondary at 120ms, corner at 190ms, with overlapping completion and a settle from 510–700ms. The choreography communicates precision through restraint, play through asymmetric overlap, and cinema through a more spacious silhouette. Candidate total durations are 540/660/760ms; endpoint angles and clearance still require a graybox. Repeated current selection does not replay. Rapid changes retarget the current pose, rather than queue films.

**B — Enter the work.** The genuine project image corresponds to the case-study hero in a proposed 420ms transition, capped at 550ms. Title and authorship become the destination hierarchy. Ordinary link semantics, direct URLs, Back and new navigation retain priority. If transition preparation is not ready within 150ms, navigation proceeds normally. The supported full experience must implement the signature; unsupported mechanisms receive a complete ordinary route.

**C — Accountable resolution.** Following a durable accepted enquiry, a 450–650ms decorative rule/fold resolves into the receipt identity. The actual receipt text and actions are available immediately. The animation cannot establish backend success or notification delivery. Unknown and rejected states retain the review composition and editable draft.

The visual spectacle comes from staging, silhouette, relative mass, partial occlusion of decorative planes, edge catches, scale correspondence and held compositions. Avoid adding effects without a reason. There is no sound or idle rotation in release one.

## A real product behind the presentation

Public routes expose home, work, case studies, approach/about, start, privacy and useful error states. Quick enquiry bypasses the longer brief. Saving a project or selecting a service changes the same draft used by the editor; it is not a decorative counter.

The draft is versioned, validates IDs, handles unavailable/corrupt storage, and exports a selected revision as accessible printable HTML and portable JSON. Name/email/message stay in session memory by default. Submission creates an immutable attempt distinct from the draft being edited. Durable acceptance, uncertain transport, actual rejection and notification status have separate meanings. An authenticated owner must be able to retrieve enquiries even when notification fails. The final decision register defines these boundaries in detail.

**Proposed architecture:** rendered React/TypeScript content; Next.js App Router only if a repository audit supports it; isolated imperative Three.js stage; scoped GSAP for authored sequences; CSS/WAAPI for ordinary UI; typed local case-study content initially. No package versions, host or transaction/notification providers are selected yet. Proposed modules include `features/hero/{assets,leaf-pose,frame,renderer}`, `features/motion/{policy,hero-bridge,navigation}`, `features/brief/{types,reducer,persistence,export}`, and `server/enquiries/{accept,repository,outbox,notify}`. These are greenfield paths, not inspected existing files.

Domain reducer owns selections/draft; route coordinator owns URL/focus; scene controller owns decorative pose; renderer owns GPU resources. One property has one writer. Recovery/degradation history survives remounts for the visit. No scene failure disables the enquiry journey.
